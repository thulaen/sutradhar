# Research families

Status: research map, not a quota or a completed inventory.

These families organise the work. A method can have several uses but keeps
one identity and one contribution to the qualified total.

- Text retrieval
  - Plain-English purpose: Find potentially useful destinations from words
    and phrases.
  - Distinctness questions: Does the scoring model change, or only its
    settings?
- Text structure
  - Plain-English purpose: Find topics, boundaries and existing phrases
    without rewriting posts.
  - Distinctness questions: Does the representation or segmentation
    procedure differ?
- Graph analysis
  - Plain-English purpose: Analyse relationships between public content.
  - Distinctness questions: Is this a different graph operation, or another
    configuration of one operation?
- Statistical confidence
  - Plain-English purpose: Describe uncertainty and calibrate scores.
  - Distinctness questions: Are the assumptions or estimators different?
- Learning to rank
  - Plain-English purpose: Learn an ordering from permitted feedback.
  - Distinctness questions: Does the training objective or model procedure
    differ?
- Ensembles
  - Plain-English purpose: Combine several outputs using a justified rule.
  - Distinctness questions: Does the combination do more than rearrange or
    reweight the same recipe?
- Optimisation and metaheuristics
  - Plain-English purpose: Explore approved settings within a measured
    budget.
  - Distinctness questions: Does the search procedure differ, or only its
    search range?
- Wrappers
  - Plain-English purpose: Study which inputs help another method.
  - Distinctness questions: Is this a distinct selection procedure, or
    another threshold?
- Meta-learning
  - Plain-English purpose: Learn how to adapt from earlier tasks.
  - Distinctness questions: Is knowledge transferred through a distinct
    learning procedure?
- Clustering and anomaly analysis
  - Plain-English purpose: Find groups and unusual behaviour for review.
  - Distinctness questions: Is the model of a group or an anomaly
    substantively different?

Every family must respect the same permissions, privacy, resource limits
and evidence requirements. A method that is useful in another domain may
not be useful here. Unusual behaviour is not proof of spam, deception or a
second account; moderation decisions remain human decisions.

## Coverage without invented methods

The required total is not divided into arbitrary family quotas. The
research backlog follows primary sources and demonstrated gaps. A family
with many names but no distinct procedures must not inflate the count.

Prioritise:

1. Low-cost, interpretable baselines for retrieval and confidence.
2. Phrase and topic analysis that preserves original source offsets.
3. Graph methods that can be evaluated without loading an unbounded graph.
4. Measurable rank-learning and calibration alternatives.
5. Bounded tuning, ensemble, wrapper and meta-learning procedures.
6. Remaining requested families and meaningful published variants.

This order does not remove any agreed release requirement. The first
complete release remains blocked until the full scope is qualified.
