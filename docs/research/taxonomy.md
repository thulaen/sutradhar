# Research families

Status: research map, not a quota or a completed inventory.

These families organise the work. A method can have several uses but keeps
one identity and one contribution to the qualified total.

| Family | Plain-English purpose | Distinctness questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text retrieval | Find potentially useful destinations from words and phrases. | Does the scoring model change, or only its settings? |
| Text structure | Find topics, boundaries and existing phrases without rewriting posts. | Does the representation or segmentation procedure differ? |
| Graph analysis | Analyse relationships between public content. | Is this a different graph operation, or another configuration of one operation? |
| Statistical confidence | Describe uncertainty and calibrate scores. | Are the assumptions or estimators different? |
| Learning to rank | Learn an ordering from permitted feedback. | Does the training objective or model procedure differ? |
| Ensembles | Combine several outputs using a justified rule. | Does the combination do more than rearrange or reweight the same recipe? |
| Optimisation and metaheuristics | Explore approved settings within a measured budget. | Does the search procedure differ, or only its search range? |
| Wrappers | Study which inputs help another method. | Is this a distinct selection procedure, or another threshold? |
| Meta-learning | Learn how to adapt from earlier tasks. | Is knowledge transferred through a distinct learning procedure? |
| Clustering and anomaly analysis | Find groups and unusual behaviour for review. | Is the model of a group or an anomaly substantively different? |

Every family must respect the same permissions, privacy, resource limits
and evidence requirements. A method that is useful in another domain may
not be useful here. Unusual behaviour is not proof of spam, deception or a
second account; moderation decisions remain human decisions.

## Coverage without invented methods

The required total is not divided into arbitrary family quotas. The
research backlog follows primary sources and demonstrated gaps. A family
with many names but no distinct procedures must not inflate the count.

Prioritise:

1. Low-cost, interpretable baselines for retrieval and confidence.
2. Phrase and topic analysis that preserves original source offsets.
3. Graph methods that can be evaluated without loading an unbounded graph.
4. Measurable rank-learning and calibration alternatives.
5. Bounded tuning, ensemble, wrapper and meta-learning procedures.
6. Remaining requested families and meaningful published variants.

This order does not remove any agreed release requirement. The first
complete release remains blocked until the full scope is qualified.
