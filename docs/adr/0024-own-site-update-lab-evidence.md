# ADR-0024: Own-site evidence for the Update Lab

## Decision

On 21 November 2026 in session 01a1f92b, the owner approved using
settled Search Console results from their own sites for the local
Empirical Update Lab. DR-119 limits each study to the active owned
site. DR-124 records the narrow exception to spec 0072.

A separately confirmed spec 0138 study may use eligible settled
comparison labels and the specified prior-window controls from the
verified Search Console property for that site. This serves local
Editorial Coach guidance and historical evaluation of that guidance.
Every ranking, relevance, detection, ranking research and release
path keeps its existing restriction.

## Reason and consequences

Comparisons can show associations on the owner's pages. They do not
identify a search system's causal rules or give a ranking guarantee.
Rival content and results, other provider outcomes and outbound
Lab acquisition are outside the exception. The source grant,
comparison readiness, exact study approval and technical admission
must each pass before the study reads real evidence.

[Spec 0137](../specs/0137-google-search-update-tracker.md)
owns the timeline and settled comparisons. Spec 0138 owns the
separate advisory contract.
