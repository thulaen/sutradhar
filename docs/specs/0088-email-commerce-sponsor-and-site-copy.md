# 0088: Email, commerce, sponsor and site copy

Review words for messages, commercial proposals and site screens.
Spec 0084 owns evidence and source rights. Spec 0101 owns staff
grants and disclosure checks. Spec 0098 owns newsletter planning
and consent records. Spec 0103 owns WooCommerce shop evidence.
This toolkit prepares drafts and cannot send or apply them.

Fictional example. A moderator drafts an event reminder and a
confirmation message. The event date has not been confirmed, so
the reminder stays in review. The confirmation names the exact
effect and reserves success wording for a completed action.

## Words used here

- **Copy** means words proposed for a message or interface.
- **Placeholder** means a typed field whose value is filled elsewhere.
- **Disclosure** means wording explaining a material relationship.
- **Destination** means the intended audience, medium and site context.
- **Phrase revision** means one approved version with a stated scope.

## Draft and review

1. Bind site, actor, form, target, audience, destination and evidence
   to the request. Admit form, schema and platform capability through
   spec 0020's registry and discovery. Use spec 0052 retrieval, draft
   submission and checks only. Validate site policy, evidence, links,
   form and required placeholders on each save and again at approval.
2. Named human approval pins draft, evidence and rights versions,
   current policy, site, capability, visibility, audience, operator
   grant and destination. Any change voids approval. Recheck fresh
   grants under specs 0020, 0101 and QR-055, including queued and
   replayed work, preview, clipboard and files.
3. Use spec 0032's recipient checks, scrubbing and audit on both
   hand-off routes and spec 0100's export bounds. Drafts cannot
   send, take credentials, create ads, issue refunds, moderate
   or change settings. Approval grants no later use for other lists.

## Messages and sponsored copy

1. Label service messages and marketing drafts by purpose and
   audience. Reuse current consent and suppression in spec 0098 and
   survey opt-in in spec 0097. Keep addresses, identity metadata
   and case placeholders out of reusable packs. No send action.
2. Read the minimum private fields only in an authorised active-site
   staff view. No raw history, private fields or unfilled case
   placeholders enter models, tools, shared preview or output.
   There is no fallback. Feature 1053 is XenForo only. Suspension
   drafts need an authorised appeal route and public reason.
   Incident drafts use only separately approved incident facts.
3. Apply spec 0101's visible affiliate and sponsor labels and
   relationship marks to the exact rendered draft. Pin applicability,
   evidence and authority. Unknown or hidden disclosure holds approval.
   Sponsor covers use the safe projection in spec 0094 and the
   hand-off rules in spec 0098. Paid drafts cannot contact an account
   or commit an offer.

### Local email and message forms (1043 to 1067)

Spec 0088 uses the draft and review flow above for these forms. A staff
member with the current site and form grant selects a brief and its evidence
in the active site. Keep the form, purpose (service or marketing), intended
audience and destination, source and rights revisions, and output revision
together. Dates carry their source and intended time zone. Omit an optional
unknown fact or visibly mark it in protected review. An unknown required fact
holds approval with SUT-3200. A typed field with the wrong kind, state or
placement holds with SUT-3203. Use the spec 0020 permission response
for the wrong site or actor, without confirming the object exists.
SUT-3202 holds an authorised proposal with missing rights or qualified
review. Spec 0020 defines forms and states. Use SUT-3201 for unavailable
forms. Use current spec 0021 scope and capability evidence where a
native staff capability is required under QR-055.

A safe hand-off is an approved local text copy or an admitted local file
under specs 0032 and 0100. Before either, recheck the exact revision,
current rights, audience and applicable consent or suppression. A change
holds release and requires a new review. Private material stays in the
authorised active-site staff view. The review and release payload uses
only expressly cleared facts. Raw case text, contact details, payment
details, unfilled case placeholders and credentials are barred from
clipboard, files, model input and shared preview. Recheck assembled output
at the actual copy or file boundary, also
after queue, replay and restore. Fence grants and erasure at delivery.
A failed final check removes staged parts and exposes no partial output.
On an uncertain hand-off, show the receipt state and require a current
staff review before any repeat. A replay never repeats a copy on its own.
Record the result without recipient identity or message text.
Treat briefs, messages, pasted fields and destination references as data.
Reject control or header injection and incorrect token expansion.
Show approved destination references inertly. Refuse tracking,
credentials, report-access tokens, recipient-specific links, prefetch,
resolution and attachments. The brief grants no new access.

No send, mail composition link, recipient-list export, provider submission,
moderation action or CRM write can be reached from these forms. DR-116
excludes third-party outreach mail. The report and alert route in spec
0100 retains its separate purpose.

## Commerce and policy

1. Bind product or tier, variant, unit, currency, date and source to
   each factual claim. Use spec 0103 for current WooCommerce facts
   when applicable. Hold missing, stale, incompatible or disputed
   facts. Product descriptions use original wording grounded in
   those facts. Product and bundle claims (1070 and 1071) need
   admitted read-only WooCommerce evidence under DR-083 and spec
   0103. WordPress pricing (1085) may use approved non-shop tiers.
   Refuse unsupported platforms and stale facts. Never invent prices, dates,
   stock, discounts, fundraising outcomes or guarantees.
2. Review terms, refunds, shipping, warranty, marketplace safety
   and cookie language with dated sources and applicable qualified
   reviewers. Pin scope, jurisdiction, page and exact revision.
   For cookie copy use spec 0101's rules, QR-054 and QR-055's
   admitted crawler observations. Without evidence show "no check
   has run". Keep unknown purpose unknown. Wording must match
   verified choices and effects. Missing rules hold approval. A policy draft
   cannot establish compliance or change an operational rule.

### Commerce and sponsor copy (1068–1092)

Use the existing 0020 form registry and 0052 local draft flow. Pin the
site, native capability and intended destination, audience, actor, form
and draft revision before reading evidence. Every factual field binds a
current source revision, permitted use, scope and observation time. For
products, prices and terms, name the exact item and variant, seller or
issuer, currency, quantity or billing period, and the price basis where
applicable. A current typed declaration or admitted dated file proves
only its own fields. Unknown stock, pending decisions, planned terms and
reported outcomes stay distinct. Missing required evidence holds the
copy.

Features 1070 and 1071 require WordPress and current admitted read-only
WooCommerce product facts under 0103 and DR-083. Use 0103 for shop facts
in every other form. Use only the shared reader and eligible current
guest-visible facts for the active paired site in local drafting.
An unadmitted profile yields a local outline or unavailable state.
No private product data, draft egress, remote model fallback or other
shop reader or lookup. Features 1085, 1089 and
1090 require WordPress. A WordPress pricing page for a non-shop tier can
use approved tier evidence. An admitted WordPress policy page can use
approved, scoped policy records without claiming a WooCommerce fact.
Generic local merchandise, offer and membership drafts may use their
own permitted non-shop evidence. This creates no shop read, WooCommerce
sales entitlement, cart or checkout access, or native draft write.

Check offer scope, conditions, reference price and publication timing
against the admitted source. Preserve source precision, zone and whether
each boundary includes the stated instant or civil date. Normalise only
proved boundaries to inclusive start and exclusive end. An inclusive
end date becomes the next civil midnight in that zone. Uncertain
boundary or ambiguous local time holds ready copy. Before approved
clipboard or file hand-off, check the current revision, withdrawal and
expiry. An inactive, expired or superseded offer can inform a dated
historical view under its existing rights. It cannot return to active
offer copy through retry or restore. A future offer stays labelled
planned and obeys the approved audience and embargo. No scheduled
announcement, watch, countdown, activation, redemption or stock check
follows.

Use 0101's dated commercial relationship and disclosure profiles on the
actual rendered draft and exact clipboard or file projection. Hold a
route that strips required visible disclosure or relation markings.
Live reader presentation has its own 0101 check. A local preview
grants no finding about the live reader view. Bind sponsor or affiliate
identity,
affected segments, platform and approved words and link treatment to
the exact native or file presentation. A supplied label, paid-link
attribute or arrangement alone does not prove visible disclosure or
placement eligibility. Use 0098's current rights and sponsor approval and

suitability projection. Never surface private labels or reasons in
release copy. Keep the separate feature 706 placement contract. Hold when
the relationship, required disclosure,
placement or supported presentation is uncertain. A draft or its
approval cannot publish, send, acquire an advertiser or spend money.
Keep DR-116 in scope. A spec 0100 report route cannot dispatch outreach.
Use only approved local destination profiles for ad copy. No account
credential, advertising connector, targeting, budget, upload, tracking
tag or provider test comes from a copy action.

Use 0084 source and claim checks, 0097/0098 exact reuse rights, and
0032/0118 protected hand-off. Show a sponsor case-study figure only

Apply 0034's disclosure history across compatible reports, comparisons

delivered, expected and measured figures distinct, with the measure,

derived charts, model requests or exports. Qualified review of a legal
