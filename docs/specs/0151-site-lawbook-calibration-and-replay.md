# 0151: Site Lawbook, calibration and replay

Figures: every numeric limit, threshold and budget in this spec is proposed
until measured on the Dell or settled by a QR.

## What it does

The Site Lawbook records what an owner has learned about a known
practice, which conditions the result covers, and what remains in
doubt. It can suggest reviewing, boosting, demoting or retiring that
practice as local guidance. Every change to guidance needs the owner's
fresh confirmation for that exact change. A study result cannot change
a site, rank a link, alter an audit or apply itself.

The [experiment planner](0148-scientific-experimentation-engine.md)
owns study and job authority. The
[estimators](0149-causal-estimators-and-placebos.md) own target effects,
suitability, falsification and statistical verdicts.
The [sources](0150-experiment-source-and-sensor-gates.md) own admitted
evidence and sensor routes. This spec consumes their sealed typed
results. It creates no collector, connector, estimator or authorised
experiment by implication. Analytics arrive only through the exact
direct API source admitted by 0150, with current rights and coverage.
No file, screen scrape, extra request or credential fallback fills a
missing analytics result. Other kinds of evidence keep their own
admitted sources.

## Shelves and decisions

Each Lawbook belongs to one registered site and explicit purpose.
The practice key binds the approved action family, mechanism, subject,
audience, target, outcome, units, window and intervention or exposure
scope. A version binds the practice definition, method profile, rights,
source policy, evidence and current authorised guidance. Identically
named practices at different sites, periods or profiles need separate
records. A description never grants authority to issue an action.

| Shelf | Exact entry condition and display |
| --- | --- |
| Open questions | Known scope and a specific unresolved question. Show missing evidence, its nearest eligible study and cost or limits if known. An unmeasured gap gives no effect, probability or priority number. |
| Under review | Exact proposed guidance change with independent review or current owner confirmation outstanding. Keep the existing approved guidance separate if still usable. |
| Supported here | Exact comparable evidence meets the current scope-specific support rule and the owner confirms the guidance version. Show effect interval, limits, assumptions and active evidence dates. |
| Evidence against | Exact comparable evidence shows a materially adverse effect or supports the declared equivalence bound. Mark which. A wide interval or a non-significant result supplies neither. |
| Contested or incomplete | A compatible result materially disagrees or a required assumption, source, scope or confirmation is unavailable. Show separate eligible results and reasons. Do not combine a disagreement away. |
| Retired | The owner confirmed retirement of one exact guidance revision. That revision stays retired. Keep the safe historical outcome and reason under current retention and erasure rules. A re-test is a new eligible opt-in plan. |

The shelf is an evidence view. Guidance has its own version, exact
approved wording, scope, decision, effective time, expiry and proof.
A result may make guidance unavailable by loss of evidence or rights.
That safety hold grants no new wording or change to another record.
A boost is a bounded preference in this practice's own advisory text.
A demotion reduces that preference or records a caution. Retirement
withdraws that exact advisory revision. A changed decision only affects
the local Lawbook and its admitted read projections. It cannot become
a scoring weight, order key, draft change, scheduled job, release gate
or mandatory-audit mute. Spec 0048 retains its separate per-kind
ordering. The Lawbook has no combined payoff score.

Pin the 0149 registered benefit threshold, harm rule and any
equivalence margins in the exact outcome units. Orient the effect so
positive means benefit. Record comparator, uncertainty level, family
treatment, windows, robustness gates and eligibility before selecting
evidence. Consume 0149's signed decision class and the exact
outward-rounded bounds used for that class. Check the rule, profile and
bound-kind identities. Never classify an effect again from a point,
unrounded bound, diagnostic interval or p-value. A benefit result
supplies no harm or equivalence verdict.

The candidate repeat profile needs 0148/0149 admission. A boost or
demotion needs at least two independent informative studies. A
retirement candidate needs at least ten independent informative harm
or equivalence studies in one registered cohort. These thresholds
permit review. Each change still needs exact owner confirmation.

1. Before any outcome or verdict lookup for the decision, seal one
   due-to-read cohort. Pin the practice and study revision roster,

   dedicated single look identity. Build the roster from eligible

   In addition to 0149's within-study checks, require independently

   In 0149 that rule uses strict benefit or harm separation and

3. A boost candidate requires two or more supported benefits and no

   qualified harm results or two or more equivalence results. Record
   the reason separately. Consider retirement only at ten or more
   independent informative harm or equivalence results with no
   eligible material benefit. Name the separate harm and equivalence
   counts. Their sum is only the count of individually informative
   studies; it creates no pooled effect, and no claim that the two
   findings are identical. Inconclusive studies never add to the
   threshold. Incomplete inventory coverage, contradiction or an
   outcome-dependent omission holds the proposal.

   each counted and uninformative status, and all admitted exclusions.

   exact eligible population and input rights, and fresh 0148
   registration. Prior measurements can be eligible frozen context

   refresh or reuse right. Under DR-146, reactivation requires a new

   support rule, and the owner's separate exact confirmation through
   the existing guidance review. The retired revision stays retired.
   Its historical evidence and safe receipt keep their existing

## Open questions and expected information

Use a binary question defined before measurement, such as whether a
qualified future replication would cross the registered benefit bound.
A separate admitted probability profile supplies the prior probability
p, two to eight mutually exclusive and exhaustive hypothetical study
outcomes, their probabilities q, and the conditional probabilities r
of the same binary proposition. Pin target, evidence, cost and profile
before ordering. Use reduced exact rational numbers, all between zero
and one. Require q to sum to one and sum of q times r to equal p.
Hold an unsupported, incomplete or inconsistent scenario set. Do not
derive p, q or r from a p-value, resemblance, language model confidence
or unknown forecast.

The information estimate is the expected reduction in squared
probability error under that profile:

`G = p × (1 − p) − sum(q × r × (1 − r))`

It lies between zero and p × (1 − p). A result outside the range is
a calculation defect. For p = 1/2, two outcomes of probability 1/2,
and r = 1/4 or 3/4, G is 1/16. This is a conditional planning estimate,
not a measured improvement or guarantee. The owner sees the scenario
weights, assumptions, sensitivity to admitted alternative inputs and
resource estimate. A unit cost needs an eligible strictly positive
approved resource estimate. Display G per unit only for the same
resource and comparable costs within this question list. Do not sum
scores across objectives or use them to order audits, links or sites.
An unresolved input leaves the question listed without a number.
Choosing a question never starts a run or changes a queue.

### Conservative display for future predictions

Per stream, outcome, site, horizon and scope, let the owner
activate one qualified calibration profile. Before their outcomes
can open, select the first 60 eligible issue IDs in canonical
issue-time and ID order. Use the registered issue-time eligibility
and independence profile. Freeze those IDs as three chronological
blocks of 20. Wait for all three frozen blocks to settle. Require
qualified independence across blocks and each comparison of
prediction and observation. An unresolved issue holds its block and the
transform. No block swaps, drops or backfills an ID. Any shorter
rights or retention deadline wins.

| Prediction | Candidate transform and same-scope checks |
|---|---|
| Binary probability | In the first block set q to (positive events + 1) / 22. Propose p′ = (p + q) / 2. Test the last two blocks separately. In each, raw Brier score must exceed the constant-q Brier score, and p′ must beat the raw Brier score by at least 1/100. Otherwise leave the supported probability unchanged. A displayed p′ is towards the observed base rate, with its own version and held-out scores. |
| Closed numerical interval | In the first block take the maximum of zero and the two signed distances below or above each stored interval, in native units. Call the maximum a. Propose [lower − a, upper + a]. In each of the two later blocks require raw coverage below its stated nominal coverage and widened coverage at least nominal. Require a > 0 and the qualified maximum display width; otherwise show the coverage warning and hold the confidence label. |

Use checked exact arithmetic. Pin the transform, support scope,
bound, expiry and evidence digests in a signed display receipt.

coverage warning. Requalification uses a later frozen roster with
no reuse of outcomes from the fit or validation. Display copy

This transform changes only the designated advisory display.
It cannot alter a rule's active state, change its weight, approve
an Action Compiler task, replace a registered causal bound or
