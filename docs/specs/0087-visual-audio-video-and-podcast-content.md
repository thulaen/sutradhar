# 0087: Visual, audio, video and podcast content

Prepare media plans, scripts, captions and rights-checked local
drafts. Spec 0084 owns the cited research pack. Spec 0097 owns asset
grants and brand presets. Spec 0096 owns manual distribution.
Spec 0094 owns report slides, separate from article slides here.

DR-076 keeps excluded outside accounts disconnected. This toolkit
cannot publish, upload or schedule provider jobs. Staff review and
use the finished files themselves. Subtitle translation is after
version 1 under DR-081.

Fictional example. Staff turn a cycle guide into a short script.
A cited chart includes an accessible table. A member photo stays
out until approved. Captions and a spoken description are reviewed
against the same video revision before local hand-off.

## Words used here

- **Rendition** means a saved version of a proposed media output.
- **Cue** means timed text or narration for part of a recording.
- **Flatten** means making one output without editable layers.
- **B-roll** means supporting footage shown alongside the main shot.
- **Text alternative** means equivalent information without the image.

## Sources, approvals and local media

Use supplied, rights-checked image assets under spec 0097. The named
outside image sources in spec 0100 are not built (DR-076).

1. Bind site, actor, capability, intended audience and destination.
   Use 0084 evidence and only spec 0052 retrieval, submission and
   checks. Check policy, novelty, links and form at save and again
   before named human approval of exact source, rendition, rights,
   policy, template and model versions. A missing or failed check
   holds the rendition. Recheck queued work, replay and manual copy.
2. Reuse the exact-use member grant in spec 0098 and asset rights in
   spec 0097. Pin all source and rendition hashes, use, audience,
   platform, expiry, credit and derivative rights. Recheck each
   grant before preview, model input, review and output. A missing
   grant withholds every affected excerpt or asset.
3. Admit and isolate each parser, codec, encoder and profile under
   QR-076. Bound input, decoded size, pixels, tracks, duration,
   output, time and queue. Strip private metadata and block active
   or external content. No reader fetches or uploads. Spec 0115's
   header reads grant no media download or conversion. Apply specs
   0032 and 0100 to safe text and file hand-off. A failed job
   leaves no partial file.
4. Raw screenshots and mask layers stay in separately authorised
   local review. Exclude them from model, clipboard, export, alt
   text, captions and derivative caches. Flatten masks, strip
   metadata and verify the encoding has no hidden material.
   Remove failed derivatives and refuse uncertain privacy.
   A suggested mask grants no export permission.

## Visual designs

1. Charts and statistic cards use protected results from specs 0034,
   0094 and 0097. Show source, period, units, denominator and missing
   values. The chart, text alternative and table use the same
   protected values. No filter, export or alternative can disclose
   a suppressed cell. An authorised same-site data change may
   automatically propose one local revision per data, template
   and accessibility version. Replays are idempotent. Refuse stale
   or erased evidence. A new revision needs fresh approval and
   cannot update a public page.
2. Use approved brand versions for templates, crops, icons, badges,
   thumbnails and watermarks. Preserve safe area, credits and
   accessible meaning. Explain mismatched before-and-after evidence.
   Validate contrast and non-colour cues under the existing design
   rules. Feature 1012 requires XenForo and an admitted capability.
3. Diagrams use a bounded, inert Mermaid subset or admitted local
   shapes. Keep equivalent structured text. Refuse unsafe directives,
   unbounded graphs and inaccessible output. The historical map
   alias F-1000 resolves only to F-0933 and has no second renderer.
4. Slide and print drafts use safe local export from spec 0032 and
   file bounds in spec 0100. No external pictures or active content.
   PDF output waits for QR-039 and the relevant QR-076 format fixtures.
