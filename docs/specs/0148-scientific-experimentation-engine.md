# 0148: Scientific Experimentation Engine

Use Experiments to test one planned change on a group of pages
you own. Before any edits, Sutradhar saves which pages will
change, which pages will stay put and what would count as a
worthwhile result. The final receipt explains what the evidence
supports, what held the test and what the next question is.

Figures: every numeric limit, threshold and budget in this spec is proposed
until measured on the Dell or settled by a QR.

## Purpose and authority

One site owns each experiment. The paid and rival research
routes have no access. Ordinary action cards and Coach advice
do not create experiments. Use **Start experiment** from an
eligible card, suggestion or the Experiments screen.

Use two visibly distinct paths. A planned randomised test uses
the 0094 whole-page or section assignment and exposure contract.
ADR-0026 records the separately approved extension.
A separate observational causal study requires its own qualified
method, predeclared assumptions and exact owner-approved
own-site evidence. Matching and placebo results alone cannot
qualify that path. Every verdict identifies its method and scope.
The site cannot prove how a search engine works or predict a
specific ranking from these studies.

An estimate describes the registered change and metric over the
registered horizon. The statistical range has its stated method
and coverage. A significance result cannot serve as a chance
that the claim is true. A separately calibrated prediction can
carry a probability for its exact forecast event.

Editorial Coach and the Action Compiler can show an eligible
experiment receipt or propose a future change. Each boost,
demotion or retirement requires a new exact owner confirmation.
Use the existing owner effect in the affected advisory module.
Product ranking, mandatory audit and safety checks, 0048 fixing,
current card ordering and 0138's existing rule and hold
contracts keep their own authorities. Experiments produce no
native edit or publish action. Site changes need their existing
authorised route and human approval.

## Plan and assignment

1. Pick one intervention and one eligible versioned primary
   metric. Register what changes and how staff will verify it.
   Examples are a single title pattern or a single introduction
   section. Reject changes that need combined independent
   alterations to interpret. A common template or shared
   component that changes both arms is a scope dependency.
2. Freeze the eligible page or independent page-group inventory
   using only pre-exposure evidence. Map exact owned native
   objects, landings and canonical pages through one admitted
   mapping revision. Pair or stratify on the pinned template,
   topic, traffic tier and pre-period trend as specified in 0149.
   A group is the analysis unit when its pages can affect each
   other. Unknown interference blocks the independence claim.
3. Display coverage, fit, detectable effect, predeclared
   guardrails and proposed schedule. See actual data and exactly
   which sources are missing. Do not show post-period measures
   or preview alternate outcomes in the balancing screen.
4. Before revealing any random assignment, the owner confirms
   the canonical population, hypothesis, dates, estimators,
   error plan and policy. Commit the assignment seed commitment
   and original plan attempt. Apply the exact eligible 0094
   randomisation once. For a non-random study, pin the known
   chosen treatment units and untouched donors before exposing
   or observing the change.
5. Seal both lists, the control weights where used, the 0150
   expected coverage and source plan, and the 0149 analysis
   plan in the pre-registration before exposure. These pin
   expected inputs and their checks. Actual final evidence
   bytes belong only to the final-look claim. Verify the seal
   at every checkpoint. Use the existing 0094
   plan and owner confirmation receipts and authenticated 0032
   audit and 0118 custody. Keep prior sealed revisions readable
   only with current scope. An unanchored digest is insufficient
   as evidence that a record was unchanged.
6. Preview the exact sealed arms to the owner. Activation
   separately checks the exposure, current roles, versions,
   applicable moderator approval and plan expiry. The tool
   cannot confirm its own proposal. Reusing the same confirmation
   key and digest returns the same receipt. A change of digest
   conflicts. The owner can abort a plan, with prior lifecycle
   and evidence retained under lawful retention. A new draft
   starts new evidence and cannot claim the previous assignment,
   elapsed window or effect.

Every plan has exact frozen date boundaries in its primary native
calendar. Let P0 be the first whole native day after a scheduled
publication and verification deadline, fixed at registration.
The primary outcome normally covers 28 full native days from P0.
Store the matching declared pre-period and any fit-only
history separately. At registration pin P0, the exact end day,
the first final-read eligibility instant, and a hard final-read
deadline. The first eligible instant is the maximum of the primary
outcome window end and each required source's applicable native
window-end instant plus its frozen profile's finality lag.
Require actual watermarks in addition to this time bound. The
initial claim deadline is seven elapsed 24-hour days from that
eligibility instant. An earlier profile, scope or rights deadline
wins. Reject a plan whose deadline cannot follow eligibility. A
new claim may enter at either endpoint of the inclusive interval
from `final_eligible_utc` to `final_deadline_utc` if every other
gate still passes. A clock tick is one UTC microsecond.
Earlier verification never advances the outcome or evaluation
dates. Late verification holds the plan without moving them.

For a separately authorised 0094 WordPress template effect, `Active`
records the approved reservation. It gives no proof of native delivery.
If the exact apply reply is lost, keep the original effect and assignment
and show “Change not yet verified” while reconciliation is pending. Use
the 0094 lease and native receipt checks. Keep the frozen dates and
withhold verified exposure and any verdict until the owning verification
and final-look checks pass. An exact historical receipt can reconcile the
original effect. It cannot renew exposure, move the dates or change a
later native edit.

The initial randomised profile intends exposure through the
end of all 28 primary post days. Native effects renew only
within the exact 0094 monitored lease and signed scope.
Record any departure against the assigned arm and follow the
pinned missingness and guardrail policy. A carry-over outcome
needs its own qualified design. A registration can never
start after the known intervention or inspect its outcome to
pick any part of the assignment.

There is one efficacy look at the end for the initial profiles.
The UI can refresh guardrails, clock, coverage and the recorded
pre-fit and calibration evidence. Published effects and ranges
come only from recorded authorised looks. A later multi-look
profile requires its 0094 frozen schedule, per-look validity
and cumulative error rule. Comparing many metrics never
substitutes for the sole registered primary metric.

### Verification and custody before measurement

Lock the read and phase dates as UTC instants alongside the named
native day ranges. Default to 28 complete post days. The resource
scheduler does not start a different exposure or change the first
or last measured day during recovery. The 0094 active-site cap
covers all experimental reservations. Default 3 active scopes.
Admission considers the declared exact measure, unit independence,
enough power for the smallest worthwhile effect, and the qualified
0149 ceilings. Present specific refusals before the owner spends
time on an infeasible plan. A different metric or intervention
needs a new plan.

Initial request bounds are 4 KiB for the inert hypothesis,
8 guardrails, 1 KB for the local intervention description and
an exact 32-byte idempotency key as hex. Hold over-limit fields
before parsing referenced evidence. Apply the narrower admitted
0149 cohort and matrix limits, 0150 source ceilings and 0104
worker profile. Ordinary non-confirmation drafts expire after
15 minutes. An exact owner confirmation uses 0020's
`actions.confirm_wait` and any sooner authority expiry.
Revoked approval and changed effects hold before any commitment.
On accepted repeat, return the current eligible receipt. Refuse
a changed input under the same idempotency key.

## Lifecycle and release

| From and event | To and exact effect |
|---|---|
| Draft, plan checks pass | Balanced. Pin eligible alternatives and pre-only envelope. A failed scope, pre-fit or power check has a safe reason. No reservation or exposure yet. |
| Balanced, exact owner confirms registration | PreRegistered. Freeze the input universe, assignment method and seed before revealing assignment. Write the sealed plan, original arm identities and one acceptance receipt atomically. |
| PreRegistered, exact approved activation | Active. Acquire the full intersecting 0094 footprint atomically. Require verified before revisions, the three current qualified activation receipts and guardrails. For separately authorised native exposure, verify the admitted sealed 2.0 site route and exact 0021 effect scope before reservation. Dispatch still requires the existing activation lease under 0094. A new effect uses its current signed intent and verified before-state. A legacy note or mismatched receipt cannot supply this proof. An earlier receipt can only reconcile its own authorised effect. |
| Active, planned exposure interval finishes | Settling. Prove exposure and continued control eligibility for the exact dates. Keep reservations through the fixed observation window and outstanding native effects. |
| Settling, clock within the inclusive final claim interval and all three sources eligible | PlaceboCheck. Atomically consume the sole look and seal a FinalLookClaim before any final efficacy read. Start the pinned placebo and efficacy jobs using only that claim. |
| PlaceboCheck, all receipts valid and one registered decision passes | Finalised. Release one immutable verdict with method, range, scope and exact gates. Spec 0151 governs whether the receipt contributes to Supported here or Evidence against. A verdict alone cannot change guidance. |
| PlaceboCheck, all receipts valid but no registered decision passes | Inconclusive. Retain the permitted estimate and interval with exact scope and reason. No positive or negative evidence classification follows. |
| Active, Settling or PlaceboCheck, confirmed shock or overlap | Contaminated. Fence scoring and release. Stop separately authorised exposure through its existing expiry and rollback. Preserve the original plan and evidence lineage. |
| PreRegistered, Active or Settling, temporary proof hold before deadline | Keep the phase and store its exact hold. Recheck a recoverable source gap against the same plan. An alert can clear only under the independent correction rule. |
| Active, Settling or PlaceboCheck, irreversible proof failure or clock after the final deadline | Inconclusive. Record source, method or gate reason. An expired or consumed look cannot be replaced. |
| Draft through PlaceboCheck, owner abort or authority withdrawn | Aborted. Bump the fence. Cancel productive work and preserve safe proof. No later output can publish. |
| Any prior result, correction or withdrawal | Attach supersession notice and withdraw its current experimental or advisory uses. A prior consumed look is not available for recomputation under a changed source. |
| Any state, project erase or uninstall deletion | Withdraw visible access first. Fence reads, adjacent release and old restore. Follow owned object ledger to verified erasure or named cleanup hold. |

At activation and final-read claim require exactly one current
qualified receipt from each named source for that phase, the
registered expected coverage and the active site. Atomically
compare state, generation, all three actual source receipts,
current authority, deadlines and contamination checks before
inserting the claim. Only its worker may read the frozen final
outcome. For a temporary interruption, keep the current claim
in PlaceboCheck. Resume only before the original deadline, with
current authority and the same ordinal, manifest bytes, source
revisions and effective checks. Wait for those exact bytes
under the existing retry limits. The retry acquires no new
source evidence. A definitively missing or changed input, or
a deadline before proof returns, makes this run Inconclusive.
Authority withdrawal still aborts. Confirmed contamination
still follows the Contaminated transition. Before release
recheck the same claim, three producer proofs, current grants,
stop and erasure fences and 0149 gates. Finalise under the same
unique look. A late worker or revised source cannot replace it.

An exact owner-approved Abort and an eligible verdict seal for the
same `PlaceboCheck` state compete on its expected state sequence and
stop fence. If Abort commits first, keep the consumed claim and refuse
any late verdict or advisory receipt. If the seal commits first, refuse
the earlier Abort under ERR-0148-STALE. That request cannot withdraw
the sealed verdict or grant a native rollback. Current access,
supersession and erasure rules still govern the original receipt.

A staged result has no release authority. If the study executor's
current grant is withdrawn after staging but before the seal, apply
the existing Aborted transition and advance the stop fence. Preserve
the consumed look and the permitted stop evidence. Withhold the late
result and its advisory receipt even when all three source proofs
remain current. Restoring the executor's grant cannot resume that
study or reopen its look.

A temporary unproved alert holds evaluation. Before the atomic
final-look claim, an independent reviewer may attest that the
matched alert was false. Bind the exact original alert, site,
footprint and interval, unchanged plan and producer profile,
new authoritative proof generation and review receipt. The
owner confirms that exact correction in the app. In one
transaction recheck the proof and authority, compare the same
generations as the look claim, append the correction and
advance the evidence fence. Continue only after all gates
pass again, within the original deadline. A true outage,
confirmed update or overlapping edit cannot clear under this
rule. After the claim, an eligible correction can only
withdraw or supersede the original result. A later test needs
a new independently approved plan.

Check locks before staging a new Action Compiler or Coach action
where it may change an active footprint. Match the exact site,
page revisions, inherited sections, templates and affected
edges. Show whether the work conflicts with a Test or Control
page. A read-only advisory action can explain the lock.
An edit request or confirmation cannot go around it. The human
can choose an unaffected scope or abort the experiment before
starting that work. Existing site changes outside Sutradhar
are not forcibly blocked. An observed or unresolved uncontrolled
change, outage, lost tag or update spanning protected dates
holds or contaminates the original plan under 0150.

Use the lease, outbox and idempotency of 0019. Pause global
productive work and evidence publication using the current
pause generation. Pause never extends the original dates,
expiry or exposure lease. Reconcile an uncertain side effect
using its original identity before resuming. A cancelled or
revoked lease cannot publish a late matrix result. Recovery
can record cleanup, expiry and authorised rollback receipts
while productive work is paused. Preserve real rollback and
recheck obligations under 0094.

## Error contracts

Codes SUT-2300 to SUT-2305 are owned by spec 0094 (DR-087); this spec only
reports them.

Use the caller's existing 0020 response envelope and the
0094 experiment result shape. Return one stable machine reason
plus permitted fields and the current snapshot identity.

| Stable reason | Exact detection and consequence |
|---|---|
| ERR-0148-AUTH | Cedar, actor, site, recipient, expiry or revoked authority fails. Apply the existing scoped 0020 denial, with no disclosure of the object or its evidence. |
| ERR-0148-STALE | Expected plan and state revision, snapshot digest, lease generation or effect mismatch. Refuse under SUT-803 and show a safe review prompt. |
| ERR-0148-PLAN | Referee finds overlap, another active plan for the same exact footprint, changed request under a used idempotency key or mutated sealed data. Report SUT-2302 and reserve no conflicting effect. |
| ERR-0148-SOURCE | One required profile has unknown, failed or incomplete evidence. Apply the matching 0150 reason and SUT-2300. Hold before efficacy evidence that would consume a look. |
| ERR-0148-HOLD | Referee finds independently confirmed overlap, lawfully withdrawn source or actual interruption before score release. Hold publication. Use SUT-2303 for a stopped exposure, and retain a permitted contamination or withdrawal receipt. |
| ERR-0148-PROFILE | The owning stats or exposure module cannot establish profile admission. Refuse the exact route with a safe prerequisite. Apply SUT-2304 when the platform route is unavailable. Missing method, verification or custody qualification is insufficient evidence under SUT-2300. |
| ERR-0148-LIMIT | Validate, planner or scheduler finds over-limit approved input, work or lease budget. Return SUT-2305 with dimension and bound where safe. Commit no partial assignment or verdict. |

## Budgets, permissions and storage

Use 0078's measured adaptive envelope. Its proposed Balanced
budget on 16 GiB is 6.4 GiB for the whole local workload.
The experiment job gets only its current admitted share.
Protect existing high-priority work. One study producer per
site may consume heavy resources at once. Run one logical
chunk per lease and checkpoint between independently ordered
fits or placebo jobs. Parallel workers may fill independent
slots on a workstation when their full concurrent promises
fit. Parallel and serial reduction produce the same canonical
values. Any GPU path needs separate exact numeric and resource
proof before admission.

Use 0104's fenced CPU, memory, elapsed time and permission caps.
Approved Python runs immutable typed numerical inputs only.
It proposes bounded results to Rust. Rust validates source,
numeric profile, gating, effect and publication. Hot reload
stages a new exact version and test evidence. A running study
keeps its registered versions. A staged tool or script cannot
replace its estimator or grant itself a permission.

All inputs remain active-owned-site protected aggregates.
Raw provider text, tokens, private paths, member IDs, sessions
and content have no place in study payloads, logs, MCP or
repeatable math fixtures. Treat all returned values as data.
Provider APIs use the named connectors and exact approved
queries through 0150. Register temporary and committed files
before data reaches disk. Protect exact storage location and
recipient under 0030/0032/0038/0118. External SSD and encrypted
S3 archives require the existing storage-wizard authority.
A past approval cannot restore withdrawn evidence or grant an
unproved cloud deletion receipt.
