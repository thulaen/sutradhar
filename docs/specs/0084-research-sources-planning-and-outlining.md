# 0084: Research, sources, planning and outlining

The MCP content toolkit helps staff research a topic and plan a
piece. MCP is the existing local connection for authorised tools.
Everything produced here is a proposal. Staff review the evidence
and use the site's own publishing tools.

This spec owns the research pack and outline. Spec 0052 owns the
shared evidence and draft intake. This toolkit reuses only those
read, submit and check steps. Spec 0041 owns the Editorial Coach,
and spec 0020 owns access. Specs 0085 to 0091 use the evidence and approval
rules here for their own content forms and checks.

Fictional example. A moderator plans a guide to winter tyre pressure.
Two threads disagree. The pack shows the exact posts and their
conditions. The moderator accepts a proposed section on the conflict.
A photo and a quote stay out of the outline until their reuse rights
are recorded.

## Words used here

- **Evidence item** means a bounded observation tied to a source version.
- **Claim** means a statement about a fact, with its scope and source.
- **Research pack** means the cited evidence table for a project.
- **Outline** means a proposed structure, with evidence and open checks.
- **Reuse grant** means the recorded permission for a particular use.

## Research pack and sources

1. Bind a project to site, topic, intended use and actor.
   Reuse spec 0052's scoped evidence retrieval, draft submission
   and checks. Read guest-visible posts, approved public WordPress
   comments, page versions and admitted aggregates. XenForo has
   no WordPress comment insight view. Raw Search Console terms stay
   under spec 0040. Own-site questions use only the separately
   admitted 0044 projection. Staff notes need a source to become
   evidence.
2. Record site, source family, passage locator or field, context,
   version or hash, observation date, purpose, rights and age.
   Staff can inspect permitted evidence locally. Unauthorised
   reusable material is withheld from packs and proposals.
   Cap and scrub before storage, display or tool access. A
   citation uses a permitted locator and version. A chosen name
   requires authorised attribution.
3. Use subject match before accepted answers, reactions and admitted
   expertise can order the source list. Explain each contribution.
   A credibility assessment names directness, corroboration, age
   and known gaps under an admitted method. Popularity alone cannot
   verify a claim. Reuse spec 0027's relevance rules for proposed
   internal citations. The pack adds no ranking input. RS29 in
   spec 0027 has no link-ranking route or feature-store field.
4. Reuse spec 0049's admitted Wikidata identity, facts and cache.
   Wikipedia contributes admitted evidence links with revision
   and date. The toolkit stores or sends no article copy and
   opens no second lookup. QR-073 owns any further source rights
   and matching admission. Use named rivals and only spec 0092's
   supplied evidence or the permitted one-off receipt under spec 0082.
   Spec 0115 limits receipts to sitemap, feed and header evidence.
   They cannot supply page-body claims or trigger a follow-up read.
5. Use protected outputs from specs 0034 and 0097. Reuse their
   admitted privacy noise without adding or rerunning it. Joined
   views, subgroups, exports and replays must still satisfy the
   source disclosure and erasure rules. Read site search questions
   from spec 0044's admitted on-site stream (job 289). Until its
   approved scrubbed view exists, show unavailable and add no
   instrumentation. Keep search-engine query evidence separate.

### Research evidence contracts (748–762)

Each item binds site, project and request, source kind and
opaque object ID, exact revision, coverage, observation time,
permitted span or fields, source rights and effective scope.
A reported assertion and a verified fact are separate outcomes.
Use the existing shared pack and no secondary archive or connector.
Apply 0020 authority, 0032 withdrawal and erasure, 0108 site
isolation and 0118 protected recipient scope at search, read,
generation, hand-off, replay and restore. 0078 and QR-073
bound scan, extraction and returned bytes. Packing a record
never grants a new use of staff notes or filed evidence.

For features 748 to 759, the
[group](../planning/feature-wave-3.md#acceptance-design-for-features-748-to-762)
names one exact variant and fixture per feature under the existing
requirements and test suites below. A shared test result covers only
the fixture and variant it exercises. QR-174 retains the source,
authority and disclosure checks before build work.

- **748, best source posts.** Start with the eligible own-site
  public topic candidates under 0027 subject relevance. Only
  after relevance, order the qualified profile by relevance
  band, current native accepted-answer event, comparable
  admitted reaction counts and separately evidenced topic
  expertise. Recheck revocation or unacceptance. For each
  auxiliary key sort known eligible evidence ahead of
  unknown, with exact approved values ordered descending.
  Use stable source ID for final ties. Show each reason. No member
  profiling, unsupported self-claim or live ranking effect.
- **749, claims.** A claim records stable source post and
  frozen original revision, exact UTF-8 byte span, direct,
  quoted or paraphrased status, origin ID and selector version,
  subject, polarity, predicate, scope, unit and date. If
  extraction normalises text, map the span back to the
  original UTF-8. Validate boundaries and digest.
  Show nearby permitted context and separate uncertainty.
  Refuse any claim without a traceable source or with a changed
  revision, and never assert independent verification from
  extraction, formatting or repetition.
- **750, disagreement.** Group only comparable claims about the
  same entity, question, unit, time and conditions. Explain the
  conflict with distinct eligible claim provenance from
  each side. Deduplicate reposted, quoted or syndicated
  copies by their original voice and source event. Unknown
  origin cannot establish an independent opposing viewpoint.
  Differences due to country, version, dates, conditions or
  incompatible units stay separate, or unknown. Distinguish
  an unsupported assertion from a verified contradiction.
  Neither popularity nor count settles which claim is true.
- **751, member quotes.** Candidate selection before reuse
  consent shows only safe opaque descriptors approved for
  review. No excerpt or attribution preview. Within eligible
  sources, order by current qualified topic relevance, direct
  first-hand support, then stable source and span IDs. Release
  only under the exact signed 658 and 0097/0098 grant. Bind
  actor and speaker, source revision and span digest, use,
  channel, purpose, credit, expiry and consent generation.
  Recheck on selection display, draft, copy, export, derivative
  and replay. Preserve wording and meaning. On withdrawal
  hold the affected suggestion and never substitute a quote.
- **752, table from thread facts.** Use an admitted closed
  typed column set. Each cell carries its value, original
  unit, exact source span and reported, verified, conflicting
  or unknown status. Preserve currency, version and time.
  Apply only a declared compatible unit conversion while
  retaining the original. Never merge unlike numbers, infer
  absent cells, execute formulas or output uncited values.
- **753, topic timeline.** Use a stable eligible source ID
  and approved topic mapping per item. Separate reported
  event date, date precision, native post time and observation
  time. Sort agreed instants with stable ID ties. A day or
  month of unknown time cannot gain an exact instant. Keep
  unsettled date or chronology separate. Edits or moved topics
  receipt a current correction, with no invisible historic
  rewrite and no display of revoked sources.
- **754, experts to interview.** Suggest only independently
  evidenced relevant expertise with an authorised public name
  and current topic-specific willingness to be considered.
  Label self-reported and corroborated evidence separately.
  Leave unclear expertise or contact willingness out. Show
  the reason using permitted sources. Ranking, a public role
  or accepting an answer does not prove a credential.
  No inferred contact details, message, booking or export
  of a person list.
- **755, research pack.** Freeze topic, content type, intended
  reader, source IDs and dates, coverage, grants and manifest.
  Include admitted 0092 queries, questions and trends with
  each unit and freshness. Reuse only the 0132
  `prepare_writing_brief` projection with `keyword.read`, the
  current brief grant and at most 20 exact snapshot/row IDs.
  Retain provenance, field timestamps, target, units, nulls,
  coverage and freshness. Never freeze a reusable grant or
  duplicate the source cache. Recheck binding, receiving scope
  and 0132 retention at every response, replay and export.
  Opt-out preserves only still-authorised retained evidence.
  Scope loss or erasure withdraws all affected use. No 0133
  access, refresh, spending or broadened provider route.
- **756, gap finder.** Reuse 0041 and admitted local 0117
  coverage for the exact draft revision and entitled evidenced
  question. Check current eligible draft and linked own-site
  coverage before suggesting the gap. Label weak, missing,
  coverage unknown and previous version separately. At pack
  delivery, replay, restore and export, recheck the exact
  question, coverage generation, linked page and recipient.
  If an eligible draft and question remain but their supporting
  linked page or coverage can no longer be used, withhold the
  dependent evidence and show coverage unknown. Keep the hidden
  target and its details out of the result. If the draft or
  question grant is lost, withhold that affected card. Require
  a new current complete check before any missing-content advice.
  A local simulated angle keeps its own source label. Identical
  wording cannot replace observed question evidence. Do not treat
  missing evidence as a gap or recommend an unapproved native
  edit or a page per phrase.
- **757, related internal citations.** Reuse 0027 same-topic
  eligibility and approved internal destinations on the active
  site. Return exact canonical target, verified visibility,
  relevance reasons and approved source/citation facts. Hold
  stale, moved, duplicate or already represented destinations
  for review. The 0119 route is separately authorised and
  excluded from this output. No automatic link action.
- **758, Wikipedia and Wikidata.** Use eligible 0049 cached
  item identity and supported statements. Keep property,
  value, qualifiers, rank, references, identity state,
  revision and date. Wikipedia contributes eligible dated
  links or separately authorised reviewer-verified claims.
  Treat these as distinct evidence. No article bytes.
  Preserve disambiguation, dispute, staleness and missing
  references. Apply existing reviewer verification and use
  rights before reusing a fact. No lookup or identity merge.
- **759, site activity.** Receive only the admitted 0034
  privacy release ID, exact site, metric, period, cohort,
  recipient, query, floor, budget generation and current
  release ledger proof. Unqualified profiles are unavailable.
  Use the fixed released value and never rerun or average
  noise. Apply one cumulative ledger across pack joins,
  prose, tables, charts, clipboard, MCP and exports.
  Suppressed cells give no exact total, denominator,
  complement or paired comparison. Keep coverage and
  noise label. Revision, overlap, scope and erasure checks
  can withdraw release and survive restore.
- **760, member photos.** Match topic and exact eligible asset
  identity from 0087/0097. A displayed image and its credit
  need current permission for the person, asset, channel and
  intended use, including each depicted person where required.
  Match only public approved labels, no identification by
  appearance. Use the inert permitted derivative and scrubbed
  metadata. An unknown subject or asset grant is a review
  item without image release. No external image fetch.
- **761, embedded videos.** Read the already admitted guest
  forum embed record from 0087 and require proved current
  topic relevance. Pin source revision, approved provider
  profile and canonical video identity. Use the earliest
  eligible occurrence by native time and stable ID ties.
  Show a safe reference and scope. Playback, download, transcription and reuse
  do not
  follow from public embed presence. Unknown host, duplicate
  instance, inaccessible source or unavailable licence is
  explicitly held. Never dereference or auto-load the embed.
- **762, earlier coverage.** Compare the exact topic with
  eligible current same-site indexed source revisions. Use
  admitted canonical and comparable text profile, topic
  relevance and approved synonyms. Keep dates, currently
  visible scope and gaps separate. Report “No match in this
  sample” when the eligible checked sample has none. Missing
  or limited index coverage cannot establish absence or let
  a draft claim to be the first treatment.

## Claims, reuse and version history

1. Extract claims with their passage, context, units or dates.
   A data table keeps its field source and distinguishes reported
   values from verified values. A timeline keeps stated event dates
   apart from observation dates. Opposing claims show both sides and
   their limitations. A gap points to an evidenced unanswered question.
2. Verify a fact only when an authorised reviewer confirms a
   current primary source for the fact or independent eligible
   corroboration. Reposts are one source. Record role, version,
   rule and date. Sparse, expired or disputed evidence cannot
   receive a verified label or support an approved claim. Use
   unscored until QR-073 admits credibility and confidence rules.
3. Reuse spec 0098's signed exact-use grant under QR-072 and
   spec 0097's asset rights. Pin source version, use, channel,
   permitted attribution and expiry. A video can reference an
   eligible embed without fetching it. Recheck at generation,
   preview, second review, copy, export and replay. Missing or
   revoked rights withhold usable text, media and attribution.
4. Save permitted extracts and notes by version. An older visible
   source is historical. Erasure, lost visibility or revocation
   immediately closes access to affected extracts, facts, status,
   notes, plans, queues, local copies and downstream caches.
   Rebuild eligible results through spec 0032 and check restore.
   Copies already taken outside the app cannot be recalled.

### Research validity and retained evidence (763–777)

Use the same bounded source and current rights projection as
748–762. Pin site, request or project, method and source revisions,
purpose, recipient, retention, and grant and erasure generations
to every output. Eligibility and privacy checks precede dates,
totals, ordering, recommendations and hand-off. No native effect,
paid connector or new crawler follows from a research action.

- **763, outdated source flags.** Compare the evidence's exact
  observation, source revision and claim-effective dates with the
  approved per-kind freshness profile. Bind the required
  time basis, native zone, precision, approved maximum age
  and trusted local time at read. Mark stale only when that
  elapsed age exceeds the bound, and superseded on a proved
  replacement. Future, coarse or missing dates that cannot
  establish age give unknown. Inaccessible is unavailable.
  Label known timestamps and exact reason. No forced refresh
  or new reading; a later approved source follows ordinary intake.
- **764, source credibility.** Assess one source for one claim.
  Use the proposed profile of four independently recorded
  checks: direct support of the exact statement, independent
  corroboration by a different original source, current
  evidence under 763, and matching scope and traceable
  revision. Submit a reviewer-visible exact source and rule
  for each yes or no. Incomplete evidence gives unknown.
  Hold on disqualifying rights conflict or a superseded
  source. Bind assessor, evidence and method revisions and
  recheck them before rendering. With all four known, score
  one point per yes and show integer 0 to 4 plus each reason.
  With an unknown, show unscored. Do not equate the score
  with probability or verification. No general person or
  domain standing, popularity weight, hidden method or ranking
  input. QR-175 and QR-073 qualify the exact rubric and thresholds.
- **765, opposing views.** Reuse 750 comparability and eligible
  source identity. Choose the strongest documented claims per
  genuinely conflicting position in this exact topic and scope.
  Order by directly supported scope, source evidence under the
  admitted method, then stable ID. Preserve conditions, caveats
  and support strength. Duplicates, quotes and syndicated copies
  do not multiply support. A missing supported opposing view
  stays unknown. No forced balance or invented argument.
- **766, glossary lookup.** Read an approved site or toolkit
  term record with exact term, alias, rule, language, definition,
  scope and version. Match literal normalised term or eligible
  alias. Warn on ambiguity or site and shared definition conflict.
  Show dated definition and allowed link. A term recommendation
  changes neither glossary nor draft. No inferred translation
  or external term request.
- **767, named entities.** Read first-party scoped entity
  observations plus the eligible 0049 cached identity projection.
  Show exact label, type, source evidence, confidence state,
  known QID and revision where proved. Use unknown for a
  name-only or ambiguous result. Preserve distinct identities
  with different proven referents. No new lookup, merge,
  identification from private fields or link ranking signal.
- **768, site search questions.** Use only the separately
  admitted 0044 scrubbed own-site projection of the job 289
  evidence. The general two-source gaps job is no question
  release. Bind exact site, on-site source, actor and purpose,
  dates, filter, normalisation scope, current release ID and
  consent/rights generation. Use the common disclosure ledger.
  Suppress personal, rare and reconstructable query labels.
  No raw query, shared search ID, member history, Search
  Console merge or new instrumentation. An unavailable exact
  projection shows “Not measured”.
- **769, WordPress comment insights.** Require a separately
  admitted native read or protected owner file for the exact
  approved own WordPress article. Bind current article and
  comment IDs and revisions, moderation and guest visibility,
  approved source and coverage. Group eligible themes and
  propose sourced changes, with scope and uncertainty. Keep
  a safe count only when allowed. A quote needs exact reuse
  rights. Exclude private, pending, removed or unproved comments.
  Erasure or changed evidence invalidates queued and restored
  outputs. No commenter identity, contact, edit or new native
  request. XenForo has no route here.
- **770, saved research notes.** Reuse 0020's project note and
  membership store. Research adds approved evidence references
  to a bounded local note with immutable version, scope and
  purpose. A reference grants no new object access. Compare
  expected revision before edits. Apply erasure and withdrawal
  to notes, citation links and derived outputs on restore.
  No independent duplicate note archive or executable content.
- **771, cited post snapshots.** Save the exact bounded extract
  and provenance under a current storage and archival review
  grant. Apply the existing extract byte and retention caps
  cumulatively to the union of retained spans per source
  post/revision, with no reconstruction via split spans.
  Bind source, span digests, actor, operation, purpose,
  destination, channel, exact signed credit, visibility and
  erasure generations. An eligible old revision may remain
  historical for the authorised archival reviewer only.
  An edit invalidates the prior 658 quote reuse for a new
  draft or hand-off. New use needs current exact rights.
  Withdrawal, visibility loss or erase closes affected
  extracts and derivatives before read, replay or restore.
  Seal and ledger files and indexes under 0030/0118.
  No whole-post shadow cache or public historic view.
- **772, citation formatter.** Use the current safe public
  locator and identity proof, exact source revision/date,
  style, claims and authorised chosen attribution. Apply the
  versioned style ordering. Refuse stale or private targets,
  unproved redirects, reassigned IDs and hidden address data.
  If signed consent requires credited attribution, withhold
  when the exact permitted credit cannot be used. Otherwise
  use an anonymous or source-only label only where explicit
  rights allow it. Check quote, name and recipient
  permissions independently. Never invent a name or follow links.
- **773, research summary.** Each sentence or claim cites a
  permitted evidence ID and source scope. Use explicit states:
  reviewed verified, supported but unverified, disputed or
  unknown. Verified needs the current 774 receipt. Supported
  means eligible direct evidence under the current method.
  Per-claim confidence is a separate versioned evidence
  assessment with reasons. A first proposed profile uses
  strong for current direct and independently corroborated
  support, qualified for one current direct source and
  limited for partially supported scoped wording. If the
  exact assessment is unadmitted, missing or disputed,
  show unscored. A verification label or 764 score cannot
  substitute. No percent without a separately qualified
  calibration. Preserve factual caveats. Withhold unsupported
  summaries and check groups before copy or export.
- **774, verified fact list.** Bind the exact statement digest,
  entity and scope, site, fact type and review recipient,
  evidence lineage, review method, instant and expiry.
  Under 0020 require a current qualified human action for
  this scope, with no MCP, delegation or automatic review.
  Confirm current primary evidence or proved independent
  corroboration. Copies or statements with a common origin
  are one source. Record an immutable attestation version.
  Check exact current source and review authority before
  admitting verification. Fence change of source, disputed
  evidence, cleared rights, elapsed expiry or erasure before
  any release. It needs a new review. Restore cannot revive
  stale verification. Retain safe eligible lineage only.
  A person's identity, an entity label, a model or a score cannot
  replace the qualified human review and its evidence.
- **775, interview questions.** Generate local questions from
  approved topic, audience and permitted same-site source cues.
  Cite the basis and distinguish directly reported question
  from a new proposal. Review scope and avoid premise claims
  without evidence or questions about a member's private
  data. Current quote rights govern any reused wording.
  Questions are drafts only. No person selection, invitation,
  booking or send through this action.
- **776, survey results in drafts.** Consume the exact 0097
  producer release ID and signed 0021 scope, including wave,
  question, metric, weighting, cohort, consent epoch, recipient
  and exact permitted output. Reuse the original approved
  protected values and uncertainty. The current signed release
  must be freshly checked before rendering, copying or showing
  managed output under the 0097 single-use 30-second receipt.
  A receipt used to render cannot also authorise copying, even
  for identical wording and figures. A receipt with time left
  still needs the current native epoch, exact recipient and
  exact use. Hold the dependent prose if any check fails.
  It cannot authorise a frozen draft after expiry or withdrawal.
  Recheck the common ledger across prose, tables, exports,
  waves, comparisons, metadata, prior outputs and complements.
  Suppressed values remain unknown. Withdraw or recompute
  after erasure. Use approved denominators only. Missing
  or biased evidence cannot support a general claim.
  No raw rows, cross-wave joins or identity disclosure.
- **777, rival coverage.** Only exact owner-named 0092 rival
  IDs, permitted supplied evidence or the existing 0082/0115
  limited one-off receipt enter this view. Recheck exact
  owner approval, use receipt, purpose, rights, expiry and
  epoch before showing derived evidence. Preserve original
  publication date only when proved by an admitted field.
  Sitemap last modification, feed updated and HTTP modification
  are their own observations. A limited receipt cannot prove
  body, earlier publication or total coverage. DR-085 leaves
  actual monitoring outside version 1. No follow-up fetch,
  private account or provider use. The 0131 and
  0133 Editorial Coach routes do not authorise this research view.

## Plans and reviews

1. Give each outline a content type, fictional audience, intent
   evidence, proposed reading goal and alternative angles and
   openings. Use spec 0092 for eligible demand. Missing demand
   keeps options unranked. Suggested actions and format choices
   state their intended outcome and evidence.
2. Each section names its purpose, proposed length range, evidence
   and open checks. A series or pillar plan orders its pieces and
   links among them. Updating a page binds its current version.
   Place a link or media asset in a plan only as a proposal.
3. Suggest interview candidates from attributable expertise, with
   consent before use. Propose questions and format from relevant
   sources. Keep contact records out of the pack. No booking,
   message or public claim follows from a suggestion.
4. Give effort as a range with assumptions. Track dependencies
   and refuse cycles. Propose cadence, refresh and promotion
   dates as local review tasks. The success measure names unit,
   baseline, population, period and unavailable evidence.
5. Show required review roles and accessible alternatives. A
   medical, legal, financial or other sensitive claim needs
   qualified human review before approval. Bind independent assistant
   review to the exact site, outline, evidence and grant versions.
   Check current owner settings for any off-PC processing under
   specs 0020 and 0052, including queued work and replay. No
   fallback. If the route or evidence is unavailable, show that.

## Failure codes

DR-092 reserves SUT-2800 to SUT-2899. SUT-2800 means evidence is
missing or stale. SUT-2801 means rights are absent or revoked.
SUT-2802 means an evidence or plan limit was reached. SUT-2803
means dependencies form a cycle. SUT-2804 means a review or source
route is unavailable. Permission errors follow spec 0020.

## Security and privacy

Check site, role, evidence rights and approved destination for each
action under specs 0020 and 0101. Treat source text and files as data.
Use existing route and owner-controlled text permissions. Logs and
external outputs carry no private source text. Toolkit approvals
authorise the pinned proposal only. Toolkit actions, including MCP,
cannot approve through automation, publish, insert links, change a
site or enqueue a publishing event.
