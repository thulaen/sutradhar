# 0085: Article types and forum-native content

Choose an article or discussion format and review a cited draft
for the active site. Spec 0084 owns evidence, rights and research
packs. This spec owns the output forms below. Spec 0052 supplies
draft intake and checks only. Staff publish through their site.
Specs 0086 and 0089 own learning pages and editing checks.

Fictional example. A moderator asks for a tyre repair tutorial and
a follow-up discussion. The tutorial shows ordered steps with a
source for each factual claim. A screenshot awaits reuse approval.
The discussion stays a separate draft with its own target section.

## Words used here

- **Form** means the required sections and checks for a draft type.
- **Draft revision** means an exact, reviewable proposal.
- **Disclosure** means a clear statement of a material relationship.
- **Moderation event** means a recorded action such as moving a post.
- **Eligibility** means the sources and site features the draft can use.

## Draft lifecycle and platform scope

1. Bind request to site, actor, form, target, purpose and the
   research pack version. Show required sections and missing
   evidence. Generate a bounded draft with sourced claims and
   an approved byline where applicable.
2. Reuse only spec 0052 retrieval, submission and checks. Check
   current site policy, novelty, links and form on every saved
   revision and again before approval. A missing policy, failed
   or timed-out check or unsupported verdict holds the draft.
   Only a named human with a current grant can approve the exact
   draft, target, policy, evidence and rights versions. Recheck
   on queued work, preview and manual copy. MCP cannot approve.
3. Admit each form's sections, platform and required capabilities
   through spec 0020's content type registry, schema and discovery
   (718, 736 and 747). Check current grants including QR-055.
   Refuse an invisible target or missing capability before reading
   sources. Feature 833 needs WordPress. XenForo forms need their
   named platform and add-ons. No automatic substitution.
4. Keep staff suggestions separate from actions. Pin, lock, merge,
   split, attach, publish, create a poll or change a label are
   site actions that this toolkit cannot perform or enqueue.
   A draft may explain a verified event or propose a staff action.

## Article forms

1. Instruction forms name audience, prerequisites, steps, checks and source
   versions. Explainers, resource lists and reading paths use their named
   layouts without required instruction steps. Screenshot steps also require a
   caption and accessible alternative. A missing step stays open rather than
   being invented. The pillar form reuses the part and link plan from spec
   0084.
2. Comparisons declare audience, matched criteria and evidence scope. Price
   and cost claims name the observed date and currency. Show unknowns on
   either side. Identify the staff test and its limits before a staff-tested
   label. Apply spec 0101 disclosures to affiliate, gifted or sponsored
   material.
3. Use spec 0084 and spec 0098's signed exact-use grant under
   QR-072. Pin site, authority, exact source and asset versions,
   use, channel, credit and expiry. Recheck before preview,
   assistant input, approval, copy, export and replay. Missing
   rights withhold usable extracts. Use specs 0032 and 0020 for
   file copies. Dialogue and Q&A require attributable source
   responses. Staff approve bylines.
4. Dated claims show observation time, event time where known and
   current scope. Predictions label their assumptions and range.
   Use protected site figures and admitted experiment records from
   specs 0034 and 0094. Distinguish member reports from measured
   outcomes and avoid causal claims without qualified evidence.

### Article form contracts (803–822)

Use the 0020 content registry and 0052 local proposal lifecycle.
Each form has an exact schema version, purpose, audience, author
and site scope, supported platform, ordered content fields,
field-level evidence, rights, and required check and approval
profile. Resolve the version, platform and current actor
before opening sources. The registry declares each required
field's type, allowed states, length, section, source count
and output bounds under the active 0052 and 0078 caps.
Treat a field as proved, reported, unknown or withheld.
An unknown required proof stays a render-only open check.
A withheld field shows only an authorised safe reason.
Never fill it with a default or misleading placeholder.
Distinguish facts, reports, labelled opinion and fiction.
A syntactically valid value gives no proof of its truth.

Use 0084 claim, verification and citation evidence. Recheck
exact source, quote, subject and asset rights under 0097/0098
before input projection, preview, generation, staff review,
queue release, export and replay. Apply owner-enabled assistant
disclosure to the exact exposed evidence. Revoked references,
staff notes and old approval cannot reach a public or model
projection. Apply 0101 to material relationships, gifted or
sponsored copy and link attributes in every preview and export.
Label source/member consent and review proof independently.
Use 0089/0091 for uncertain or sensitive claims. A missing or
changed required proof, grant, type or reviewer holds release.
Apply 0032 and 0118. No native effect.
