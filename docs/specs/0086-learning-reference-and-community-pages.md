# 0086: Learning, reference and community pages

Prepare lessons, reference material and community guidance as cited
drafts. Spec 0084 owns evidence and reuse rights. Spec 0085 owns
article and forum forms. Spec 0052 supplies evidence retrieval,
submission and checks only. Staff publish outside this toolkit.

Spec 0087 owns media production. Spec 0098 owns newsletter plans.
Spec 0101 owns account, rights and compliance controls. This spec
can explain those controls and cannot change them.

Fictional example. Staff plan a beginner cycle maintenance course.
The prerequisites name which checks learners should know. A reference
card has sources and readable text. A safety note stays in review
until a qualified reviewer checks its exact wording and scope.

## Words used here

- **Objective** means something a lesson expects learners to practise.
- **Prerequisite** means a skill or lesson needed earlier.
- **Reference row** means one scoped fact and its evidence.
- **Jurisdiction** means the place whose rules apply.
- **Procedure revision** means the exact approved steps and version.

## Learning and assessment drafts

1. Bind form to current site, actor, capability, target, purpose and
   cited pack. Admit layout and platform in spec 0020's registry,
   schemas and discovery. Reuse only spec 0052 retrieval, draft
   submission and checks. Check current site policy, novelty, links
   and form on save and named human approval. Missing or failed
   checks hold the draft. Pin draft, target, evidence, rights and
   destination. Recheck grants at preview, replay and hand-off.
2. Lesson and course drafts name audience, objectives, conditions,
   prerequisites and activities. Mark optional and required steps.
   Refuse a dependency cycle and offer an accessible ordered list.
   A next-lesson recommendation uses approved objectives and
   prerequisites, without a new learner profile.
3. Separate prompts, rubrics and solutions. Public reveals may use
   admitted spoiler rendering. A spoiler is not access control.
   Keep staff-only answer keys separate and authorised, out of
   learner views, clipboard, exports, model inputs and their caches.
   With unsupported spoiler rendering, offer separate review only.
   The toolkit creates no ballots, marks, certificates or profiles.
4. Email lessons form a dated local sequence under spec 0098's
   manual policy. Flashcards and print materials use safe local
   exports under specs 0032 and 0100. There is no send or enrolment
   action. Discussion drafts cite only eligible threads.

### Learning and assessment form contracts (893–922)

Use the exact admitted 0020 type and 0052 local draft lifecycle.
Bind site, actor, audience and purpose, target and platform,
course/lesson ID and revision, objective IDs, prior conditions,
source pack, rights, expiry and review. Map every activity,
exercise or cited solution to an eligible objective. Give
missing evidence a scoped open check. Keep content in the
approved local draft only. No publish, enrol, delivery,

Reuse 0084's dependency algorithm and stable order. The
learning graph uses its own admitted lesson count under
`learning.lesson_limit`, from one to 80, with a proposed 240
edges. The 0084 planning-only 64-part bound applies when
that distinct planning profile is used. Apply the stricter
0078 envelope at allocation. Bind lessons, activity results,
objectives and prerequisites to exact revisions. Reject
duplicate IDs/edges, cycles, stale or foreign dependencies.
Missing or hidden required prerequisites hold every dependent
lesson transitively. Optional steps cannot remove them.

without names, counts or hints about hidden roots.

Store question, expressly public answer, staff-only answer
and educator guidance as distinct scoped records. Keep keys
and private notes under separate current purpose and object
grants, with no inheritance from course access. Project each
current revision before rendering, comparison, caching,
model input, clipboard or export. Never leak private keys

changes display. Unsupported reveal goes to local review.

data. Recheck grants throughout cache, replay and restore.
