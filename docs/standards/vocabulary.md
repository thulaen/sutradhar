# Vocabulary

The words code modules and internal names may use (AGENTS.md section 4:
code modules may carry Indian names; everything a person sees on screen
is plain English). Carried from the earlier work's naming draft of
3 November 2026, and approved by the owner as the words agents may use
on 9 November 2026. A module's name must also be in the
[glossary](../glossary.md), which the gate checks.

Each word has its spelling in Latin letters, in Devanagari, its English
meaning, its part of speech and a rough guide to saying it.

Three words replace earlier ones, to avoid near-collisions:

- `pratishtha` (reputation) replaces `karma`, keeping `karya` for jobs;
- `abhilekh` (record) replaces `lekha`, keeping `lekh` for a post;
- `agvani` (reception, welcome) replaces `namaste`.
