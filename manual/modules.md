# Modules

This page is for owners who want to know what a part of Sutradhar
does and where to check its results. Use the modules admitted for
your release, site and PC. Leave an item marked **Not yet available**
at its stated limit.

## Numerical work

**What it does.** Ganit helps compare possible links and runs
scheduled calculations for the app.

**What to check.** Read the job result and the reason shown on the
suggestion. If a simpler local fallback was used, the suggestion
says so. An offline comparison can provide evidence for a later
decision. It cannot change the setting used for live suggestions.

### If a processor rollback is held

The app can use the PC's processor without a graphics card if that
setup passes its own checks. In Model Hub, check the restored model,
its search index and the device actually in use. The files and the
processor setup can pass while the rollback itself stays on hold.
Read the reason in the original rollback result before trying again.
New suggestions can return after the app verifies the rollback and
shows which set is in use.

## Visits and analytics

**What it does.** Darshak reports eligible visits and shows the
coverage of its counts. Counting follows the site's current privacy
choices and permissions.

**What to check.** Read the source and date range before comparing
figures. A missing or unmeasured interval gives no visit total.
If traffic looks unreliable, the app can keep the last eligible
traffic rates for ranking and show why.

## Accounts and reports

**What it does.** Hisaab prepares ledgers and reconciled reports
from permitted cost records in scheduled batches.

**What to check.** Check the site, period and currency in a statement.
Read the reason for any item awaiting resolution. A failed batch
keeps the last verified output. Spending limits belong to the service
that incurs the charge.

## Keys and approved outside services

**What it does.** Tijori keeps the keys used to verify and protect
Sutradhar's work. An approved outside service can supply only the
data or storage allowed for its own purpose.

**What to check.** Use the stated local unlock or approved key change
if trust is unavailable. For a provider, check the site, destination,
approval and charge before continuing. See
[Configuration](configuration.md) for local agents, resource settings
and outside-service controls.

If Tijori has stopped signing because it cannot verify recovery,
follow the owner's recovery plan in the owner tool. Tijori resumes
signing only after it verifies both signature indexes and the
corresponding recovery record. Leave signing stopped while either
is unverified.
