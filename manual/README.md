# Sutradhar manual

For owners and site administrators. These guides explain the exact
setup and recovery steps admitted by the release you use.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) explains verified installation,
  connecting your site and reading its setup checks.
- [Using Sutradhar](using-sutradhar.md) covers site readiness, the
  job list, suggestions, draft checks, optional search lookups and
  the Google update timeline.
  - [Site administration](site-administration.md) covers native access,
    review, pause and checking the outcome of a save or undo.
  - [WordPress safety](wordpress-safety.md) explains supported content,
    the protected admin connection and exact undo.
- [Modules](modules.md) explains the main tools and their results.
- [Configuration](configuration.md) covers settings, resource budgets
  and approved connections.
- [Operations](operations.md) covers backup, restore, updates, rollback
  and exact managed removal.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) covers failed checks, uncertain
  results and safe preparation of a support bundle.
- [Reference](reference.md) explains where to find your release's
  settings, action help, errors and changes.

Use the version of this manual that matches the verified release.
Where a step is unavailable for that release or platform, stop at
its stated limit.
